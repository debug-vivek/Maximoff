🤖 Maximoff

Your Personal AI Assistant

Maximoff is an AI assistant project created to make interacting with AI simple, useful, and natural.

The idea behind Maximoff is to have one place where you can ask questions, get help with coding and learning, brainstorm ideas, solve problems, and interact with AI through a modern interface.

I’m building Maximoff as a personal AI project and continuously improving it by adding new features and making the overall experience better.

✨ What Maximoff Can Do

- 🤖 Have AI-powered conversations
- 🎙️ Support voice-based interaction
- 💻 Help with coding and programming
- 📚 Assist with learning and understanding concepts
- 💡 Help generate ideas and solve problems
- ✍️ Assist with writing and everyday tasks
- ⚡ Provide a simple and responsive experience
